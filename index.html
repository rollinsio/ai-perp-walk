AI Safety Only Needs a Perp Walk

Handcuffs could heal our AI safety woes.

The running count of reported felony-grade incidents per system, and who answers for each.

What counts. Each tally is the number of entries in the docket: lawsuits, criminal and regulatory investigations, court orders, national bans, and misuse the companies themselves disclosed. Each entry is a distinct proceeding or reported event. An entry is an allegation or a report, not a conviction. Click a number to read the coverage and judge for yourself.

Add a case. Edit data/incidents.js and open a pull request. Every entry needs at least one link to news coverage.

Photos and logos via Wikimedia Commons, rendered in grayscale. See ATTRIBUTION.md.

Disclaimer. All cases are unresolved unless otherwise indicated. Everyone is innocent until proven guilty.

This site is commentary and opinion. Every entry summarizes reporting published by the linked outlets; the summaries are not independent findings of fact and may contain errors. A lawsuit is an allegation. An investigation is a question. A company disclosure is that company's account.
