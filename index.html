AI Safety Only Needs a Perp Walk

The law is not missing. The prosecutions are.

Handcuffs could heal our AI safety woes.

The running count of reported felony-grade incidents and government actions per system, and who answers for each.

What counts. Each tally is the number of entries in the docket: lawsuits, criminal and regulatory investigations, court orders, national bans, and misuse the companies themselves disclosed. Only the model is on trial here. An entry has to be about the model: how it was trained, what it generated, what it did, or what people did at its behest. The company's data centers and business dealings don't count. Each entry is a distinct proceeding or reported event, and an allegation or a report, not a conviction. Every entry quotes a statute. Either the conduct, if the allegation is true, is a felony, or a government has brought a civil action under the statute cited, in which case the entry is marked civil. Private civil suits that don't reach a felony stay off. Dismissed cases are out. Shady isn't enough. Click a number to read the coverage, the law, and judge for yourself.

Add a case. Edit data/incidents.js and open a pull request. Every entry needs at least one link from a source on the trusted list.
